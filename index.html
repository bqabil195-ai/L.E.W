ABOUT LANDHI ENGINEERING WORKS

Established in the heart of Landhi Industrial Zone, Karachi, L.E.W has grown from a specialized metal fabrication shop into one of Pakistan's most respected OEM component suppliers for thermal management and suspension solutions.

OUR METALLURGICAL & THERMAL LEGACY

For over three decades, Landhi Engineering Works (L.E.W) has maintained an unyielding commitment to material purity and structural integrity. In high-demand thermal management systems, pure copper and brass remain unmatched in heat dissipation, repairability, and resistance to environmental stress compared to aluminum counterparts.

Simultaneously, our Spring Division utilizes state-of-the-art furnace heat treatment, precision eye-rolling machines, and automated shot-peening bays to manufacture suspension assemblies that withstand extreme load capacities across rough terrain.
